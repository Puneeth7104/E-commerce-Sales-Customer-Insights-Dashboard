6. Recommendations

  1. Rebalance towards margin. Electronics is the biggest category (₹2.46 Cr, 45% of sales) but earns only 10.3% margin vs 53.2% for Beauty. Cross-sell Beauty to Electronics buyers and review Electronics discounting.
  2. Fix or prune Books. It brings only ₹5.78 L (1.1% of revenue) at 19% margin — bundle it or cut slow SKUs.
  3. Buy quality, not volume, in acquisition. Email customers repeat at 61% vs 38% for Paid Ads. Shift budget towards Email and tighten Paid Ads targeting.
  4. Protect Champions. 1,329 customers (24% of base) generate 57% of revenue — add loyalty tiers and early access.
  5. Win back At-Risk buyers. 489 formerly frequent customers have gone quiet; a time-limited win-back email is the cheapest revenue available.
  6. Grow beyond the core. South and West deliver 65% of revenue. East and North-East are under-penetrated — test local campaigns and free-shipping thresholds.
  7. Attack returns where refunds concentrate. 8.0% of orders are returned (₹48.25 L refunded); Electronics is 43% of refunded value.

Method

Order, customer, product and location tables were merged into one order-line table. Cleaning steps are logged below. Revenue counts Delivered orders only; Returned orders are treated as refunds and Cancelled orders never complete. Net revenue = quantity × price × (1 − discount); margin uses unit cost. Repeat-customer rate = customers with 2+ delivered orders. Customers are segmented with RFM quartiles (recency, frequency, monetary).
